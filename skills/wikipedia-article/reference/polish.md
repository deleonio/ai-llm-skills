# polish — Final quality pass

Runs **after** content is stable: audit verdict *ready* or *ready with revisions*, no open Blockers or Majors. Never polish a draft that may still be deleted or restructured — polish refines, it never conceals a redesign.

Read `STYLE.md` if present; this is where the profile has the most room.

## 1. Preserve

The pass may touch prose only. Untouchable: facts, figures, citations and their attachment points, section structure, neutrality semantics, attributed evaluations, quotes (exact wording), wikilinks. If a content problem surfaces mid-polish, stop and route it back to `revise` — do not fix it silently in passing.

## 2. Triage

Work in this order:

1. **Encyclopedic tone** — remaining peacock and weasel wording, editorializing, reader address.
2. **Machine tells** — patterns that make prose read AI-generated (the Advocate also flags these, see reference/audit.md):
   - Copula avoidance: "es gibt", "fungiert als", "dient als", "bringt … mit", "übernimmt die Einbindung" → plain "ist/hat" plus the concrete verb.
   - Stiff synonyms: "verstarb" → "starb", "verfasste" → "schrieb", "leistete Unterstützung" → "half". The simple word wins.
   - Vague object verbs: "Strukturen für X mitbringen", "die Einbindung übernehmen" → name what actually exists or happens.
   - Pronoun-open chains ("Sie …, Es …, Diese …" in consecutive sentences) and rhetorical colon appositions ("X mit: Sie …").
   - Mechanistic paragraph openers ("Darüber hinaus", "Zusätzlich" at every start), trikolons, forced synonym rotation, participle-I add-ons ("…, wodurch die Bedeutung unterstrichen wird"), dash accumulation.
   With `vermenschlichen` installed, its rules take precedence — this list is the fallback.
3. **Precision** — vague verbs ("deals with", "engages with") replaced by what actually happens; fuzzy quantities tightened to what the source says; terms used consistently (one concept, one term).
4. **Concision** — redundancy, filler starts ("It is noteworthy that"), doubled information, nominal style unwound. Cut without losing a fact.
5. **Flow** — sentence connections, paragraph topics, rhythm per `STYLE.md`. Vary sentence openings; short sentences for load-bearing facts.
6. **Formatting** — consistent date, number, and unit style; italics and bold per convention; link targets sane.

## 3. Bounded passes

At most two full passes. Pass 1: triage levels 1–3. Pass 2: flow and formatting. Then stop — endless polishing burns trust and introduces drift. The article must read effortless, not polished-over.

## 4. Verify

After each pass, check that no citation moved, no fact changed, no evaluation gained or lost attribution. A polish that changes meaning is a defect.

## Output

- The polished draft.
- A change log of the strongest upgrades, each as before → after with a one-line reason — this is the coaching part: the writer should absorb the moves, not just accept them.
- One line stating the passes run and that meaning and citations were preserved.
